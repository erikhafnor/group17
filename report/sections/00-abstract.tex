Hospital medical-technology departments need an overview of how medical devices fail in use,
and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration's MAUDE database is the largest public source of such
reports. The raw files are hard to use correctly: they arrive as cumulative, annual and monthly
add and change files, in a legacy encoding, with records that break across lines and change
files that re-deliver existing reports. We build a batch pipeline in PySpark that ingests these
files into a Bronze, Silver and Gold (medallion) architecture, resolves report versions, enforces
and measures quality rules, and serves monthly product- and manufacturer-level tables for a
device-safety dashboard. The pipeline runs on a schedule through GitHub Actions with separate
development and production environments.
